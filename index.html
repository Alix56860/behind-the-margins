The stories behind the numbers

How do businesses really make money, and what’s left after the bills are paid?

Behind the Margins is a student-led podcast exploring business models through conversations with founders, CEOs and business leaders. We unpack revenue, costs, pricing and profit margins to understand what makes a business work.

From familiar brands to unexpected ventures, discover the decisions, challenges and trade-offs behind the numbers.

For students, aspiring entrepreneurs and anyone curious about business.

How an episode runs

Each conversation follows the money through a single business, from the first sale to the last line of the accounts.

    Money out

    The biggest costs, and the ones people don’t expect.

    The margin, and the decisions that shape it.

Markup is not margin

Markup compares profit to what the product cost you. Margin compares profit to what the customer paid. Mixing them up is one of the most common pricing mistakes.

A profit and loss statement takes a year of sales and subtracts costs in layers. Each layer has its own margin, and each one tells you something different about the business.
